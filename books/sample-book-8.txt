RAIN ON TIN

The roof keeps a drum for every storm,
and plays it badly, and plays it well.

I lie awake and count the beats
the way a sailor counts the swell.

LAUNDRY

Sheets on the line like slow white sails,
the wind a patient hand.

Whatever the sea of the morning holds,
the yard is a kind of land.

THE LAST BUS

Windows fogged with other people's breath,
a stranger's head against the glass.

We are all travelling the same dark road,
and each of us thinks we are the one to pass.

KETTLE

It starts as a whisper, builds to a cry,
then settles back into steam.

Some mornings are only this:
a small warmth, and a dream.

INVENTORY OF A POCKET

One key, one stone, one ticket stub,
a button, a coin, a thread.

How much of a life is carried around
in the things we have not said.
